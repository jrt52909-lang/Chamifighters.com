How to play

Knock them out

Move & blockUse the stick on the left. Push left or right to walk and pull down to block. You always face your teacher, and blocking cuts most of the damage.
Attack Punch: quick, chain three for a finisher Backpack: slow, knocks them down and breaks a block Pen: thrown from range
Jump & Flyer Takeoff Jump over low shots, spills and shockwaves The meter fills as you hit and get hit. When full, tap it for a four-hit combo. You cannot be hit while it plays.
Every arena fights backSpills in the STRC, rolling dodgeballs in the AAC, wind in Gold Star Stadium, bell shockwaves in the Chapel and falling stage lights in Darby. Department chairs hit harder and take longer to beat.

On a computer: AD move, S block, W or Space jump, J punch, K backpack, L pen, U special, P pause.
